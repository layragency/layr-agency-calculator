Jika skor ini tidak mencerminkan beban kerja sebenarnya dari brief Anda, silakan sampaikan kepada brand responsible Anda. Masukan Anda membantu menjaga sistem poin tetap akurat dan adil.

How the score is built

Every brief starts at 1 point. Duration adds the most and counts differently per format, editing complexity is second, and the rest add a little. The total rounds to the nearest point (.5 rounds down), from 1 to 5. Points measure the brief, not the editor's speed.
